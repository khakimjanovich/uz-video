# Series B: Build an acquiring system

A course that teaches a backend developer who is new to payments to build an e-commerce card acquiring system from zero: ecom, the payments product, and the card zone, a separate PCI DSS unit that is the only place a card number lives.

- **Lessons:** 38, in the order their concepts depend on each other, in 9 modules. Each lesson is one concept, 4–7 minutes, 16:9: why (~20%), how it works (~40%, the build map and an evolving diagram), build it (~40%, real code, config and commands on screen).
- **Ids:** `c-NN-<slug>` under `studio/src/reels/`.
- **Briefs:** one per lesson, written and checked against the code; each video is made from its brief: script → voice → stills.
- **Rules:** the current design only, never history or plans; one home per concept; generic names (card processing centre, card scheme, issuer), no bank or centre names on screen; configured values said as configured; no test, CI or bug stories.
- **Old episodes:** the earlier `yt-*` episodes are kept at tag `series-b-episodes` for reuse of their diagrams and steps (`git show series-b-episodes:studio/src/reels/yt-NN-…/steps.ts`).
