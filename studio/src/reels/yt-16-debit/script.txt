What stops two clicks on the pay button from charging the card twice? Two confirms arrive at the same moment, and both try to debit. An atomic update moves the payment from open to authorizing, and only one caller can win it. The winner resolves the terminal, and freezes the commission for this payment. Every call to the processing center is written to an operation journal first. Then the adapter authorizes, which places a hold on the card. Then it captures, and the money really moves. On approval, the payment becomes captured, with the scheme's reference number, the RRN. A state machine allows only legal moves, so a captured payment can never go back to open.
