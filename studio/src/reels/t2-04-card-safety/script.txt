How does a card number stay safe on its way to the bank? The simplest way is that the store's website collects the card number and sends it to the bank. But then every store holds card data, and under PCI DSS that makes each one a target and a liability. So the card is entered only on the bank's hosted payment page, opened by a one-time session link. The bank's token vault turns the card number into a token, and the payment keeps only a masked PAN. The real number is revealed only inside the bank, at the moment of the debit. To prove the payer owns the card, a one-time password goes by SMS to the card's phone. Resends are limited and spaced by the configured time, and wrong attempts are limited too.
