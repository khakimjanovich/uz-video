How can ecom take a card payment without ever seeing the card? In a real shop, the card reader on the counter belongs to the bank. You type your PIN into it, and the shop only gets a receipt. Our payment page works the same way. When a buyer opens the page, ecom asks the card zone for a card-entry session: a short-lived place to type one card for one payment. The card zone answers with an address. The page shows that address inside a frame: the bank's card reader, on ecom's counter. The buyer types the card into the frame, and the card goes straight to the card zone. The page gets back only the token and the masked number. Then ecom asks the card zone to charge the card by its token, and checks that the token really came from this payment's session. The two products agree on every question they may ask each other, written down once: a contract. ecom can only ask what the contract allows. And that token can spend money, so ecom still guards it, like a ticket that can fetch a coat. Next: how every service gets its settings.
