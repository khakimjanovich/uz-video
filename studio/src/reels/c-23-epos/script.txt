Through which machine does an online payment go? A taxi cannot take fares without a licence plate, and a plate is issued once and never reused. Each online payment goes through a virtual card machine, an e POS terminal, and its plate is a pair of numbers the card network's centre issues: the merchant id and the terminal id. A bank admin registers the pair for one website and one card. There is only one live terminal per website and card, and a pair is never registered twice. Where a centre requires it, the terminal is registered with the centre too, and kept only if the centre accepts it. Every change is written down. And before every charge, the payment asks for the live terminal. No terminal, no charge, and no money moves. Next: how the bank knows a request really comes from the merchant.
