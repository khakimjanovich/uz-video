How do you stop a website taking more money than it is allowed? A concert hall can never sell its last seat twice, even with two clerks at the counter. A website's payment limits work the same way: the most it may take per payment, and the most per day. A payment over the per-payment limit is refused the moment it is created. The daily limit is harder, because five buyers can pay at once. So each charge reserves its amount on the day in one step, and only if the total still fits. Racing charges wait their turn. A charge that does not fit fails before any money moves. And in front of it all, the edge accepts only modern encrypted connections and modest request sizes.
