Should everyone at a merchant see and do everything? In a shop, the owner, the accountant and the cashier each have their own badge, and each badge opens only the doors that job needs. A merchant's team works the same way. Each person has their own login and one of four roles: admin, accountant, operator, or observer. An admin invites people. Each one sets their own password and an authenticator app on their phone, because these people can give money back. Every action names the roles that may run it, and nothing more: least privilege. Changing someone's role or blocking them takes effect at once. And a merchant can never lose its last admin, so nobody is ever locked out of their own shop. Next: the merchant's websites.
