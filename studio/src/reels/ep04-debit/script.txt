The buyer opens the payment link, so what happens to their card next? The card is entered on the bank's own page, and the bank swaps it for a token, and the tokenization deep dive lays bare why a breached database yields no card numbers. An SMS code goes to the card owner's phone to confirm it's really them. The bank then debits the card through the processing center using authorization and capture, while dedicated deep dives on timeouts and declines trace where the money goes when things go awry. When the processing center approves, the payment is captured.
