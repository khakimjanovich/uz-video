How does a merchant give money back? Back to the tab book. Before the day closes, you cross the line out: a reversal. After that, you hand the money back: a refund. The day decides, counted in the bank's own time zone, and the whole amount always goes back. The merchant can give back a paid payment within its refund cap, and a later refund must also be within its refund window. One claim lets only one caller win, the logbook is written first, and then the card zone is asked. Refused leaves the payment paid. No answer is asked about later, never sent twice. Beyond the cap or the window, the bank refunds on request, and one admin asks while another approves. Next: following one payment through the whole system.
