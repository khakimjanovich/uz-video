How does a new merchant earn the right to take card payments? To get a driving licence, you hand in your papers, each one is checked, you get a licence with its own number, and every step goes in your file. A merchant earns its states the same way. First it uploads its company documents, because the bank must know who it does business with: know your business. The bank reads each file's real type from its contents, not its name, and keeps a fingerprint so a changed file is caught. A bank admin checks every document. Only when all are verified can the company be verified, and then it signs a contract with its own unique number. Every move between states is written down: from where, to where, who, and why. So years later, anyone can see how a merchant became what it is. Next: why switching a merchant on needs two people.
