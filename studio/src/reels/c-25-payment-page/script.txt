How does a buyer reach the bank's payment page, and nobody else? A hotel key card opens one room, only for the guest who holds it, and only until checkout. The payment page works the same way. The merchant sends the buyer a link with a one-time key. The bank keeps only a fingerprint of that key, and the link expires. The page shows the website, the amount, and the cards it accepts. It opens a card-entry session with the card zone and puts the card frame inside. The page listens only to the frame, and accepts only a token from it. ecom checks the card belongs to this payment, and then the charge starts. And a double click never runs twice: one lock lets only one step through at a time. Next: the charge itself.
