Why should one person never switch a merchant on alone? Some company cheques need two signatures, so one person alone cannot spend the money. Switching a merchant on works the same way: four eyes. One bank admin, the maker, opens a request. Nothing changes yet. A different admin, the checker, approves it, and only then is the merchant active. The maker cannot approve their own request: the screen does not offer it, and the server refuses it anyway. A request that nobody decides in time expires. On approval, the documents are checked again, in case something changed meanwhile. And the history names both people, the one who asked and the one who approved.
