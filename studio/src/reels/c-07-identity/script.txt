How does the card zone know a call really comes from ecom? Entering an office building, you show an ID card from the building's own pass office, and reception gives you a day pass that lists the floors you may visit. The card zone does the same for every call. The first proof is a certificate signed by the card zone's own pass office. Both sides show one, so each knows who is on the other end: mutual TLS. The second proof is an access token from the card zone's own login server. Inside the token are roles: the floors you may visit. Each operation needs its own role, so even the real ecom can only do what its roles allow: bind a card, read it, or charge it. Steal one proof, and you still get nothing. The card zone keeps its own pass office and its own login server, separate from ecom's, so nothing outside the room can hand out its passes. Next: how the card zone talks to every card network.
