When does a module deserve its own service? The card vault holds card numbers, so it needs stricter security, and its own audit scope. Because it only ever talked through its interface, we can move it out cleanly. The in-process call becomes a REST call, behind the very same port. It gets its own database, and its own deploy. The payment flow works exactly as before, now across two services. That is the whole system, built part by part, from one map.
