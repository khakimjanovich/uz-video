How much does the bank earn on each payment, and who decides it? Fees can't be hardcoded, because every merchant negotiates its own. So commission rules live in their own module, each with a scope, a card scheme, and a validity window. A rule for one sales point beats a rule for the merchant, which beats a rule for the whole card scheme. Within the same scope, a priority number breaks the tie. The payment module asks only one question: what is the fee for this payment? If the fee would eat the whole amount, the payment is refused. Changing a fee is a maker-checker request, so nobody changes money rules alone. Every change is kept in a history table, and old payments keep the fee they were charged.
