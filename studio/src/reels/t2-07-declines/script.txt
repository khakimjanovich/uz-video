Declines: what happens when the processing center says no? The simplest way is to show an error and end the payment. But many declines are easy to fix, like a card without enough money, and the buyer would simply leave. So a decline comes back with a decline code, and the buyer can try another card while the session is open and attempts remain. When the attempts run out, the payment is closed as declined. Sometimes the money is already held, but the capture is declined. Then the bank voids the authorization hold, so the buyer's money is released. It then checks the status again to confirm the hold is gone, and if it is not, the workflow takes over.
