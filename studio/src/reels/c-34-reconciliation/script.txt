How does the bank know its records match the card network's? You check your bank statement against your own records, tick each matching line, and circle every difference. Every day, for each card network, the bank does the same: reconciliation. Our side is every money movement we recorded. Their side is the processing centre's own report, with masked card numbers only. Lines are paired, and each pair must agree on whether money moved, the amount, the terminal and the day. Every difference is a break. A break becomes an exception, with an owner and a deadline. A person resolves it with evidence, or asks to write it off, and someone else decides. So no difference is ever silently ignored. Next: the last seat in the concert hall.
