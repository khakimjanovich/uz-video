What do you do when the bank's network never answers? Your parcel left, but tracking says nothing. You do not post a second parcel. You check tracking, waiting a little longer each time, and before the post office closes, you go and ask in person. An unknown charge is never declined, and never sent again, because the money may already have moved. A durable workflow remembers it, even across restarts, and asks the network only about this one charge, waiting longer each time. Found means paid. Not found means declined. Found after the buyer's page has closed, the money goes back. It stops before the day's deadline, and anything still unknown goes to manual review, where a person decides.
