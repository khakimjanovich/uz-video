A buyer wants their money back: is it the same today as next week? The simplest way is one send-the-money-back button for everything. But before clearing the money has not really moved yet, and after clearing it already has, so one button cannot handle both. Before end-of-day clearing, the store makes a reversal: the full amount is cancelled and the buyer is never charged. After clearing, the store makes a refund instead, and the money goes back as a new operation. A refund can be full or partial, and several partial refunds are allowed, but never more than the captured amount. Refunds go only to the original card that paid. They are allowed within the configured refund window, which the bank can extend for a sales point that gives a warranty. Both can be started from the merchant portal or through the store's API.
