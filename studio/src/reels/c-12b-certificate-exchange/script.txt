How does ecom get a certificate from the card zone without handing over its secret key? To get a passport, you send an application with your photo. The passport office checks it and stamps it. It never needs your face, only the photo. A caller does the same. It makes its private key in its own Vault, where the key stays. It sends only an application: a certificate signing request. The card zone's certificate authority, its passport office, checks that the caller is on its list, and signs it for a limited time. One folder comes back: the certificate, the office's own seal so the caller can recognise the real card zone, a secret for the login server, and the addresses to call. The caller stores the folder in its own Vault. No private key ever crosses between the two products. And every certificate is watched, so a warning goes out well before it expires.
