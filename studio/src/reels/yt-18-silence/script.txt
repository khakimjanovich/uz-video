What happens when the processing center goes completely silent? A debit with no answer is unknown: the money may have moved, so it is never declined, and never sent again. Someone has to ask the scheme later, and keep asking, even if the app restarts in between. So each unknown debit gets a Temporal workflow, and Temporal keeps its waits durable. The workflow only asks for the debit's status, and waits longer each time: one second, then two, four, up to a minute, eight times at most. It asks with the same reference the debit carried, our payment id, so the answer is about this debit and nothing else. A Humo hold still waiting is captured as its own step, and only then is the payment paid. Found makes the payment paid, not found makes it declined, and the journal records the answer. Not found counts only after the debit call's time limit, because until then the scheme may simply not have it yet. Found after the buyer's page has closed, the order is over, so the money goes back with a reversal. The checks always end before the scheme's next clearing cut-off, less a margin, while a reversal is still possible. Still no answer, and a person decides: the payment goes to manual review, and the merchant hears nothing yet. In the bank's console, staff see every payment left without an answer, check it with the scheme, and record debited or not, with a required note. Their decision follows the same rule: not debited is declined, debited is paid, or given back if the page has closed, and each one is audited with who made it. The workflow's id is the payment's, so starting it twice keeps the first. A sweep every minute starts it for anything left unknown, and for debits the app stopped in the middle of. If Temporal itself is down, the debit still ends unknown, and the next sweep starts the workflow. When the page stops waiting, the payment records that the buyer is owed the result by SMS. The dashboard counts how unknown debits ended. A gauge counts the payments still waiting, unknown or in manual review, and an alert emails when they wait longer than the configured time.
