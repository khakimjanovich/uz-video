What happens when the processing center goes completely silent? A debit with no answer is unknown: the money may have moved, so it is never declined, and never sent again. Someone has to ask the scheme later, and keep asking, even if the app restarts in between. So each unknown debit gets a Temporal workflow, and Temporal keeps its waits durable. The workflow waits, asks the scheme, and waits longer each time: thirty seconds, then one minute, two, and up to ten. It asks with the same reference the debit carried, our payment id, so the answer is about this debit and nothing else. Approved makes the payment paid, declined makes it declined, and the journal records the answer. After twenty four hours with no answer, a person decides: the payment goes to manual review. The workflow's id is the payment's, so starting it twice keeps the first. A sweep every minute starts it for anything left unknown, and for debits the app stopped in the middle of. If Temporal itself is down, the debit still ends unknown, and the next sweep starts the workflow. The buyer's page keeps checking, and turns paid when the scheme finally answers.
