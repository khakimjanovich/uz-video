The processing center never answered: was the money taken or not? A timeout does not mean failure, because the debit may have gone through. Retrying blindly could charge the buyer twice. So the payment is marked unknown, and a durable workflow takes over. Temporal asks the processing center for the status, with backoff, and survives restarts. If the debit went through, the payment is captured, and if not, it is reversed. If the answer never comes, the payment lands in a manual review queue. A sweep checks every minute for payments stuck for too long. Next, telling the rest of the world what happened.
