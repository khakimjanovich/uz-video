The payment went through in seconds, so when does the money reach the store? At the end of the day, the processing center settles all card payments with the bank. The money lands on the store's terminal account at the bank. The bank keeps its fee and moves the rest to the store's own account by the next banking day, and the commission and refund deep dives shed light on how the fee is set and how money makes its way back to the buyer. The store also gets a daily report to match every payment with its books.
