How does this laptop setup become a production system? First, we build a container image for the app. A Helm chart deploys it to Kubernetes, with three replicas behind one service. Actuator's liveness and readiness probes tell Kubernetes when to restart a pod, and when to send it traffic. Secrets come from Vault at startup, and never from files. With three replicas, every scheduled job would run three times, so ShedLock lets only one of them run. Kill a pod in the middle of a payment, and the payment still resolves exactly once. Next, moving one module out into its own service.
