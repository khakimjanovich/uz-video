How do you make sure a card is charged exactly once? At a bank's cash desk, only one cashier can hold the withdrawal slip, and the cashier writes it in the logbook before opening the drawer. The charge works the same way. First, one claim lets exactly one caller win, however many clicks or retries arrive. The winner finds the live terminal and the fee. Without either, the payment fails, and nothing is charged. Then it writes the charge in the logbook, before any network is asked. ecom asks the card zone to charge the card by its token, with an id that makes the charge happen once, even if it is sent twice. Some networks only hold the money at first; for those, the card zone takes it at once. Approved means paid. Declined is final. And no answer in time is unknown, never declined.
