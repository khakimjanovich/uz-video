Your bank holds Aziza's account, and she sells shoes online. But when her customers pay by card, the money goes to Payme and Click, not to you. With its own acquiring, your bank earns a commission on every payment. The rest stays in your bank too, as deposits. Next to Aziza's real shop, a site selling iPhones for fifty thousand soum looks just as real. Two bank employees check every shop, so only Aziza's real shop can take payments. A hacker copies Aziza's real payment and changes the amount to ten million. Every request is signed with Aziza's secret key, so the forged copy bounces off. A network glitch makes her shop send the same payment twice, and her customer could be charged twice. Your bank charges the customer only once, and Aziza keeps a happy customer.
