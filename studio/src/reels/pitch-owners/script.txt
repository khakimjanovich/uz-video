Every card payment in your city skips your bank. Your bank earns a commission on every payment. The rest stays in your bank too, as deposits. Before any store goes live, two bank employees check its documents and the sanctions lists. A fraudulent or illegal website can't take payments through your bank. If anything slips through, your bank shuts it down in seconds. Every payment request is signed with the store's secret key, and works only once. If a request is sent twice, the customer is still charged once. When in doubt, your bank says no.
