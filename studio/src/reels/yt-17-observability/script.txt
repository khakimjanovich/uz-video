When a payment is slow or stuck, how would you even know? One payment crosses five modules and a processing center, and plain logs cannot follow it. Micrometer counts payments by status and times every processing call, and Prometheus collects those numbers. Grafana turns them into a dashboard, with alerts on timeouts. OpenTelemetry gives every payment a trace that follows it from module to module. The logs carry the same trace ID, and go to Loki, right next to the dashboards. Now a stuck payment shows up as a spike, and its trace shows exactly where it waits. Next, what to do when the processing center goes completely silent.
