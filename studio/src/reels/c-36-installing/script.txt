How do you make sure no single person can open the bank's secrets? Tear a treasure map into five pieces, and give them to five people. Any three can put it together, but no one alone can. Vault is opened the same way. At install, it is split into five key shares, and any three open it. Each keyholder keeps their own share, and nothing of the ceremony is written down anywhere. After every restart, Vault is sealed again, and three keyholders must unseal it. A master key for setup is made only for that session, and always revoked at the end. And because the database backups are unreadable without that Vault, the two are always backed up together. Next: one install, everywhere.
