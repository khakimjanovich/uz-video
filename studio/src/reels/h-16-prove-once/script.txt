How many times a year should a bank look at your face? For Aziz, this year, the answer is once, and after that only when something new happens. In lesson fifteen, Aziz passed a full proofing in the mobile bank: his document and his face, compared with the state records. This lesson shows what the platform keeps from that day, so that the next check is short, and so that most days have no check at all. Start with what MyID gave back, next to the identity. After a successful proofing, MyID issues a reusable identifier for Aziz. It is valid until a date in the contract between the bank and MyID, for example the end of the month or the end of the year. The platform keeps the identifier and its expiry date with Aziz's verified identity. It never sends it to the phone. Eight months later, Aziz's phone falls into a river. His identifier is still valid. He buys a new phone, installs the mobile bank, and types his number and his password. The new-device code arrives by SMS, through TAF, and the verdict is green. His old phone, at the bottom of the river, cannot approve the login, so after the configured waiting time the correct code is enough. Now the platform sees a customer who was at level two, on a device that it has never seen. So it asks for MyID, but not for everything. This is a re-check. Aziz passes the consent gate again, because every call to MyID needs it. The platform opens the session with the reusable identifier already set. The MyID screen skips the document, and goes straight to the camera. MyID checks that the face is live, and compares it with the person behind the identifier. It sends back a match result, and no profile. The platform keeps the match confidence, the references and the time, and the identity data does not change. The new phone becomes his trusted device. It took Aziz one look at the camera. So which kind of check comes when? The platform decides, from the purpose and from the identifier. A step-up from level one to level two is always a full proofing: there is nothing to compare with yet. A refresh of the identity is always a full proofing too, because its job is to read new data. A new device, or a dormant account, of a customer who was at level two, gets a re-check while the identifier is valid. When the identifier has expired, it gets a full proofing with the personal identification number already set, so the screen still goes straight to the face, but the platform receives a full profile. A yellow verdict from TAF, for a customer who was at level two, follows the same rule: a re-check before the login continues. And one more rule: the platform never sends a customer at level two, with a valid document, to MyID for any other reason. No channel, no app and no partner can start a proofing for him. I think this rule matters more than the re-check itself. Every call to MyID costs the bank money, and every extra face check teaches customers to show their face whenever an app asks. Prove once, then bind. The platform keeps no face template, and it runs no face engine of its own. MyID checks that the face is live, and the platform keeps only the result. What about the side apps? Aziz also logs in to the marketplace app on his new phone. A side app never asks for MyID at login. His marketplace session starts at level one, and it gets level two only after he approves it in the mobile bank. Prove once also works inside a single journey. Suppose that a step-up stops right after the result from MyID arrives, because the app closed. Within the configured reuse period, the platform completes the step-up with that same result, with no new call to MyID. After the period, a new proofing is necessary, and a result is only ever used for its own purpose. To recap: the reusable identifier stays in the bank, a re-check compares only the face, a step-up and a refresh always read the full profile, and nobody may send a proofed customer to MyID for nothing. So the platform now holds Aziz's verified identity: his names, his document, his addresses and his personal identification number. That is the most sensitive record in the whole system.
