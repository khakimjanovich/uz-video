Before a store takes its first payment, what does the bank check? The store registers, confirms its email, and uploads its company documents. The bank reviews them, signs a contract that fixes the commission, and records the store's settlement account. The commission deep dive will unpack exactly how every fee is calculated. Each website becomes a sales point with its own API key, and the bank checks that the site is real. Then the bank switches the store on after a second admin approves it, the four-eyes principle, which its own deep dive explores in depth, revealing why one admin alone is never enough.
