What happens when a buyer says, I never bought this? A small-claims court keeps one file per case, a date for your evidence, and a ruling. A dispute works the same way. It comes from the bank's own customer, or as a claim from the buyer's bank through the card network. Bank staff register it on the paid payment, and the merchant is told. They ask the merchant for evidence by a deadline counted in banking days, which skip weekends and the bank's holidays. Won, and the payment is paid again. Lost, and only with a second admin's approval does it become a chargeback: the money goes back to the buyer. Every step is written down. Next: checking our books against the card network's.
