How do we know whether a card is Uzcard or Humo, and where do we keep its number? The first digits of a card, its BIN, tell us the scheme. A scheduled job keeps the BIN registry in sync with both schemes. A plain card number in our database would be a disaster on the day of a breach. So the card vault encrypts the number with AES-GCM, and gives everyone else a token instead. Only the vault can turn a token back into a card number, and only to send it to the processing center. Every reveal is written to an audit trail. A stolen database now holds nothing but ciphertext and meaningless tokens. Next, the payment itself, starting with a request nobody can forge.
