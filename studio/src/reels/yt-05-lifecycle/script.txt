Can a merchant jump straight from signing up to taking payments? Without rules, one wrong update could switch on a shop nobody ever checked. So a partner moves through fixed states: pending, verified, waiting for a contract, approved, and active. A guard allows only the legal moves, and an illegal jump is rejected with a clear error. The admin verifies the documents, and the bank signs a contract with a unique number. Every change is written to a status history, so we always know who moved what, and when. An active merchant can be suspended and restored, or terminated for good. Every later check in the system asks the same question: is this merchant active? Next, we make sure no single admin can switch a merchant on alone.
