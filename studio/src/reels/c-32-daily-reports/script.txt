How much does the merchant get today? At closing time, a shop counts the till, and counting the same day again gives the same answer. Every night, the bank does that for each website. A business day runs from midnight to midnight in the bank's time zone: the same day that decides reversal or refund, so the two never disagree. It adds up what was paid, the fee, what was refunded, and the payout. The payout can even be negative, when refunds outweigh the day's sales. Counting a day again replaces it, never adds to it, so a missed night is simply caught up. And behind every total is a list of every line, so any number can be explained.
