How do you tell the world about a payment without ever losing the news, or inventing it? Picture an app that saves a payment as paid, and then tells the shop. If it crashes in between, the shop never hears. Swap the order, and a crash announces a payment that was never saved. An office solves this with an outbox tray: you close the file and drop the letter in the tray in one motion, and the postman collects from the tray. When a payment ends, its news is written to the outbox in the same moment as its final state: both or neither. A relay is the postman: it collects the oldest unsent news and passes it on, one payment's news always in order. It marks a letter sent only after it is delivered, so a crash can send one twice, never zero times. Every piece of news has its own id, so a repeat is easy to drop.
