The bank has known Aziz for six years. He has a salary card there, and a deposit that he often forgets about. heeyID has known him for one week. Are they talking about the same man? In lesson eighteen, the phone check gave Aziz the status Confirmed, and with it level two and a trusted phone. This lesson takes the last step of the step-up: linking, the answer to one question, is this proofed person a customer of the bank? Two records, in two systems. The heeyID person record is about identity: the phone number, the password, the level and the verified identity. The bank's customer record is about money: accounts, cards and products. Linking connects them with one thin thread. When the status becomes Confirmed, the platform sends one value to the bank's customer lookup: the personal identification number. Nothing else about Aziz leaves. The lookup has five answers. Found and active: the platform attaches the bank customer identifier, and the link state is Linked. That is Aziz's answer today. Not found: the link state is Not linked, and the platform sends an event that the bank can use to start its own account opening. Opening an account is a process of the bank, not of the platform. Found but closed or blocked: the link state is Refused, and an operator gets a case. More than one found: also Refused, and also a case. And no answer within the configured timeout: the link state is Pending. The platform keeps the bank customer identifier, and nothing more. If the lookup answers with accounts, products or balances, the platform deletes those fields before any other step. A reference, never a copy. Why so strict? A copy is old on the day it is made, and every copy is one more place to leak from. The lookup answer may also carry a name and a date of birth, only for comparison. The bank's records spell Aziz's family name in an older Latin spelling. The platform records a comparison note on Aziz, and on the link event. The difference alone does not stop the step-up, and it does not change the link state. Now the rule that, I think, a tired developer will most want to break. There is no operation that links by phone number. Not in the gateway, not in the admin panel, and not for an operator. The lookup accepts only a personal identification number. Why? Because mobile operators give old phone numbers to new persons. The number that Aziz used at university may belong to a stranger next year. A link by phone would one day attach that stranger to Aziz's deposit. A phone number is a contact channel, not an identifier. What if the lookup is down? Imagine that Aziz is confirmed on a night when the bank's customer system is in maintenance. His link state is Pending. The platform tries again at the configured interval, during the configured period, and records each result. After the period, the state becomes Refused, with the reason: lookup not available, and an operator gets a case. Meanwhile, Aziz keeps his access grant. His level is two, and the link is a separate fact. The mobile bank reads the link state in the identity summary, and decides which banking functions to show. A refused link can still end well. An operator who resolves the case can enter the bank customer identifier, after the bank checks it. The link becomes Linked, with the mark set by operator, and with the operator, the time and the reason. To recap: one value goes out, one identifier comes back, each answer has its state, a mismatched name gets a note, and nothing ever links by phone. Aziz's step-up is complete: level two, confirmed and linked. Module four ends here. From now on, his phone carries all of this.
