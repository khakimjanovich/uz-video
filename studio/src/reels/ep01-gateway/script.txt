What does an online store in Uzbekistan need to accept card payments? By law, only banks and payment organizations licensed by the Central Bank can connect to card processing centers like Uzcard, Humo, Visa and Mastercard, not stores. So an acquirer has to sit between the store and the processing center. A bank acquirer takes the card on its own secure page, and the store never sees the card number.
