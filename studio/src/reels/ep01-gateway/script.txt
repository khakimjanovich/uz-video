What does an online store in Uzbekistan need to accept card payments? By law, only banks and payment organizations licensed by the Central Bank can connect to card processing centers like Uzcard, Humo, Visa and Mastercard, not stores. So an acquirer has to sit between the store and the processing center. A bank acquirer takes the card on its own secure page, and the store never sees the card number. The store signs one contract and gets paid into its own account at the same bank. From here, the series follows one payment from start to finish. First onboarding, then creating the payment, the card and the debit, the status, payment links, and finally settlement.
