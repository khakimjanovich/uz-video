A buyer wants their money back: is it the same today as next week? Before settlement, a reversal cancels the payment, and the hold simply disappears. After settlement, a refund sends the money back as a brand new operation. Refunds can be partial, and the payment moves to partially refunded. The total refunded can never be more than what was captured. A refund is allowed only within the refund window agreed in the deal, the one we stored on the sales point.
