May every website of an approved merchant take payments? A market licenses the company, but every stall is still inspected on its own. Each of a merchant's websites is a sales point, and it starts as a draft that cannot take money. Its return addresses must be secure and on its own site, so a payment can never send the buyer somewhere else. A bank admin checks the site and records the result. The deal says which cards the website takes, and how much it may take per payment and per day. Only with an active merchant, a passed check and at least one card can the website be switched on. And before taking any money, every part of the system asks one question: may this website take a payment right now? Next: how the bank's fee is decided.
