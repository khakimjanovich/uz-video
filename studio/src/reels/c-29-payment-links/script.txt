How does a seller with no website take a card payment? Some sellers take orders in a messenger, and have no website code at all. For them, a payment link is like a bill with a pay here button. A merchant admin or operator makes one in the portal, for one of its live websites: an amount, what it is for, and how long it works. Behind it is an ordinary payment, and the website's limits still apply. The link is shown once, and the bank keeps only its fingerprint. The buyer pays on the same bank page as any other payment. And a link nobody pays in time is closed. Next: giving money back.
