A merchant may run several websites, so which one is allowed to take payments? A sales point is one website, with its own settings, card schemes, and allowed return addresses. The deal also fixes how many days refunds are allowed, and a longer window needs a written reason, such as a warranty. It starts as a draft, and cannot take payments yet. Two admins check the site: a real shop, real goods, and a legal business. A failed check records its findings, and the sales point stays closed. A passed check lets the sales point go active. While it is active, the merchant cannot quietly change the website address. The payment module will ask one internal question on every request: is this sales point active? Next, a second module appears, and with it, copied code we must remove.
