How do you follow one payment through the whole system? A merchant calls: my customer's payment has been spinning for a minute. The payment crossed the payment page, ecom, the card zone, an adapter and a card network. Without one number to follow, someone searches four places and guesses. In a hospital, your wristband number is on every chart, in every department. Here, every request gets one trace number that travels with it across both products, and every answer names it, so a caller can quote it to support. Every log line carries the same number, so a warning leads straight to the request that caused it. And every line the running system writes is cleaned first: card numbers keep only their first six and last four digits, and secrets are hidden. Each product sends all of this to its own monitoring. Dashboards show how payments end, and alerts send an email when payments stay unknown too long. Next: what the merchant gets each day.
