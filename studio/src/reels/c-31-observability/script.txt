How do you follow one payment through the whole system? A merchant calls: my customer's payment has been spinning for a minute. The payment crossed the payment page, three modules, the card vault, an adapter and a card network, on four machines. Without one number to follow, someone searches four log files and guesses. And the log lines that would help are the ones most likely to hold a card number. In a hospital, your wristband number is on every chart, in every department. Anyone can follow your whole stay by that one number. Here, every request gets one trace, and its trace id travels on every call, across both units. Every response names that id in a header called X request ID, even when the answer is a refusal. So a caller can quote it to support. Every log line carries the same id, so a warning links straight to its trace. And log masking cleans every line the running app writes. Card numbers keep only their first six and last four digits, tokens their last four, and secrets are hidden. Each unit sends all of this to its own Grafana, and the card zone runs its own. A dashboard shows how payments end, and which are still unknown. And alert rules, written as code, send an email when payments stay unknown too long. Now read it in the code. One small filter runs before security, so even a refused request carries its id. It takes the current trace, and writes its id into the response. The masking keeps the first six digits, stars out the middle, and keeps the last four. And any run of thirteen to nineteen digits is treated as a card number, wherever it appears.
